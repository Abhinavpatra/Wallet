
Built a Wallet Generator App for Solana and Ethereum. Here's a quick video walkthrough.

The app uses:
- `Next.js` for the UI.
- `TypeScript/tsx` for the language.
- `bip39` for generating secure mnemonic phrases.
- `bip32` for hierarchical deterministic wallet derivation.
- `bip44` for standard wallet derivation.
- `ed25519-hd-key` for Solana wallet derivation.
- `ethers` for Ethereum wallet derivation.
- `bs58` for Base58 encoding.
- `Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, headlessui` for styling.

Developed by diving into the documentation of these libraries to ensure robust functionality.
